Everything about taro frappes

Taro is a root that tastes sweet and a bit nutty. Blend it with milk and ice and you get a thick, creamy drink with a natural purple color.

Where does the flavor come from?

From cooked, mashed taro. It's sweet without being bitter, so it goes great with milk, coconut, vanilla or fruit.

How is it made?

We blend taro with cold milk and ice until it's thick. Then we adjust the sweetness and top it with cream or whatever you like.
